About Code & Conflict

Pioneering cyber warfare education through research, analysis, and community engagement.

Our Mission

To demystify cyber warfare and empower individuals with knowledge to navigate the digital battlefield.

Founded in 2025 at the University of Sindh, Code & Conflict emerged from the growing need for accessible, high-quality cybersecurity education. Our platform bridges the gap between academic research and real-world cyber threats.
