Common Email Threats

Phishing Attacks

Fraudulent emails designed to trick recipients into revealing sensitive information.

Example: "Your account will be suspended unless you verify your credentials"
Received: from suspicious.domain.com (192.168.1.100)
by mx.google.com with SMTP id 123abc;
From: "Your Bank" <support@yourbank-fake.com>
Reply-To: attacker@phishing.com

Malware Attachments

Emails containing malicious files that infect systems when opened.

Example: "Invoice attached" with a .js or .exe file
  • Unexpected attachments
  • Double extensions (.pdf.exe)
  • Password-protected archives

Business Email Compromise

Impersonation of executives to authorize fraudulent transactions.

Example: "Urgent wire transfer needed" from "CEO"
  • Slight domain variations (ceo@your-company.com)
  • Urgent requests bypassing normal procedures
  • Requests for secrecy

Link Manipulation

Embedded links that redirect to malicious sites.

Example: "Click here to view document" with hidden URL

Hover over links to see actual destination. Watch for: https://yourbank.com.evil.com

Email Security Checklist

  1. Enable two-factor authentication (2FA)
  2. Implement SPF, DKIM, and DMARC
  3. Use strong, unique passwords
  4. Disable automatic image loading
  5. Verify sender addresses carefully
  6. Enable email encryption (PGP/SMIME)
  7. Regularly review forwarding rules
  8. Educate staff on phishing awareness
